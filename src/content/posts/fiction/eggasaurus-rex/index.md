<p><em>-Author’s Note from the Wobbleverse.</em></p>
<p><em>This wobbly, glitter-splattered tale is a joyful break from my usual writing. Eggasaurus Rex is my very first attempt at a children’s short story, filled with sock trees, meatball disasters, and one delightfully ridiculous hero. I&#39;d say it&#39;s meant for kids somewhere under the age of 9.</em></p>
<p><em>It’s meant to be read out loud, giggled through, and shared with young (or young-at-heart) readers.</em></p>
<p><em>So if you&#39;re here for my darker fiction or grown-up stories... today, we wobble instead.</em></p>
<p>------</p>
<p>One morning in the Land of Wobblebottom,</p>
<p>right between the jellybean jungle and the sock tree forest,</p>
<p>the sky gave a hiccup.</p>
<p><br /></p>
<p>BOING. BONK. SPROINGLE-SNAP... THWAP!</p>
<p><br /></p>
<p>A rainbow-striped egg came bouncing down Trampoline Mountain,</p>
<p>ricocheted off a giggle-bush,</p>
<p>and landed smack in the middle of town square,</p>
<p>right next to Grumpasaurus’s soup stand.</p>
<p><br /></p>
<p>It wobbled.</p>
<p>It rolled.</p>
<p>It hummed a little disco tune.</p>
<p><br /></p>
<p>Tina Triclipclops dropped her clipboards.</p>
<p>“All three!” she gasped.</p>
<p>“That egg is unregistered and smells like marshmallow glue!”</p>
<p><br /></p>
<p>Zoomy Zaptor zoomed,</p>
<p>spun seventeen loops,</p>
<p>and flopped face-first into a muffin tree.</p>
<p>“ZOOOOOOM-SPLAT-CHA-BOING!”</p>
<p><br /></p>
<p>Baby Bloop put a sock on the egg</p>
<p>and sang it a lullaby.</p>
<p>Then floated upside-down into the bushes, whispering,</p>
<p>“You shall be Sir Socky von Wiggle, our bravest knight.”</p>
<p><br /></p>
<p>The egg wiggled...</p>
<p>jiggled...</p>
<p>jellied...</p>
<p><br /></p>
<p>And with a glittery wobble-pop,</p>
<p>it burst like a confetti cannon full of jellyfish and socks.</p>
<p><br /></p>
<p>SPARKLES. EVERYWHERE.</p>
<p>They got in noses.</p>
<p>They danced on foreheads.</p>
<p>One sparkle flew up and gave the moon a high five.</p>
<p><br /></p>
<p>Out rolled a perfectly round, giggling dino</p>
<p>with disco-ball eyes, banana peel slippers,</p>
<p>and lettuce taped to his tail.</p>
<p><br /></p>
<p>“HELLO, TOTS!” he chuckled.</p>
<p>“I’m not a dinosaur. I’m a wiggly-jiggly Eggasaurus Rex-a-roo!”</p>
<p><br /></p>
<p>He hiccupped jellybeans.</p>
<p>Three birds fainted.</p>
<p>One tree applauded.</p>
<p><br /></p>
<p>Tina cleared her throat.</p>
<p>“Dino Orientation, Rule One: Roar like you mean it.”</p>
<p><br /></p>
<p>Eggasaurus puffed his cheeks,</p>
<p>scrunched his sparkly face,</p>
<p>and ROOO,</p>
<p><br /></p>
<p>AH-CHOOOO!</p>
<p><br /></p>
<p>A blast of glitter launched Zoomy into a lemonade stand.</p>
<p><br /></p>
<p>“Rule Two,” said Tina, wiping glitter from her glasses.</p>
<p>“Stomp like you’re angry at the ground.”</p>
<p><br /></p>
<p>Eggasaurus raised one banana-slippered foot.</p>
<p>“STOMPY-STOMP!” he shouted.</p>
<p><br /></p>
<p>whooooosh.</p>
<p><br /></p>
<p>He slipped, tumbled,</p>
<p>and rolled into a pineapple cart.</p>
<p><br /></p>
<p>Grumpasaurus Grumble looked up.</p>
<p>“Hmph. That’s not a stomp. That’s a squish with ambition.”</p>
<p><br /></p>
<p>“Let him chase something!” Zoomy yelled.</p>
<p><br /></p>
<p>Eggasaurus nodded.</p>
<p>He locked eyes on a butterfly,</p>
<p>waddled after it…</p>
<p><br /></p>
<p>and hugged a cactus.</p>
<p><br /></p>
<p>“OOOOPS-A-NOODLE!” he said happily.</p>
<p>“Wrong butterfly.”</p>
<p><br /></p>
<p>The other dinos stared.</p>
<p><br /></p>
<p>Tina sighed.</p>
<p>Zoomy groaned.</p>
<p>Grumpasaurus muttered, “He’s not even a real dinosaur.”</p>
<p><br /></p>
<p>Eggasaurus blinked.</p>
<p>His glitter dimmed just a little.</p>
<p>“Oh,” he whispered. “Maybe I’m not.”</p>
<p><br /></p>
<p>He rolled into the bushes,</p>
<p>quiet for the first time,</p>
<p>and sat beside Sir Socky von Wiggle.</p>
<p><br /></p>
<p>Sir Socky didn’t say anything.</p>
<p>But his stripey silence was very comforting.</p>
<p><br /></p>
<p>Suddenly, the ground jiggled.</p>
<p>Then wiggled.</p>
<p>Then made a slurping noise.</p>
<p><br /></p>
<p>“Oh no,” said Tina.</p>
<p>“It’s happening again.”</p>
<p><br /></p>
<p>Every dino turned to the cliffs.</p>
<p><br /></p>
<p>A GIANT, GARGANTUOUS SPAGHETTI MEATBALL BOULDER</p>
<p>was barreling toward town.</p>
<p><br /></p>
<p>It had pepperoni eyes.</p>
<p>It had garlic bread legs.</p>
<p>It had NO brakes.</p>
<p><br /></p>
<p>“EVERYONE FOR THEMSELVES!” Zoomy shrieked.</p>
<p>“GLUE ME TO THE EARTH! I AM A TREE!”</p>
<p><br /></p>
<p>“We need a plan!” Tina shouted.</p>
<p>“We need soup,” said Grumpasaurus.</p>
<p>“Bloop,” said Bloop.</p>
<p><br /></p>
<p>Eggasaurus peeked from the bushes.</p>
<p>His eyes widened.</p>
<p>Then… he smiled.</p>
<p><br /></p>
<p>“Can I ride it?”</p>
<p><br /></p>
<p>“NO!” everyone yelled.</p>
<p><br /></p>
<p>He grinned.</p>
<p>“Then I’ll roll at it!”</p>
<p><br /></p>
<p>He curled into a perfect rainbow ball...</p>
<p>spun up speed...</p>
<p>and launched himself straight at the meatball boulder.</p>
<p><br /></p>
<p>BOING! ZOOM! SLAMMO-RAMA! KA-BLOOSH!</p>
<p><br /></p>
<p>He stuck to it.</p>
<p>He giggled.</p>
<p>He waved at a butterfly.</p>
<p><br /></p>
<p>Then,</p>
<p><br /></p>
<p>he rolled left,</p>
<p>rolled right,</p>
<p>bounced off the sock tree,</p>
<p>spun around Muffin Hill…</p>
<p><br /></p>
<p>and SPLORRRRRRSH!</p>
<p><br /></p>
<p>Straight into Great Tomato Lake.</p>
<p><br /></p>
<p>Noodles flew.</p>
<p>Cheese rained from the sky.</p>
<p>Bloop caught a meatball in her sock.</p>
<p><br /></p>
<p>A tiny mayor popped out of a muffin</p>
<p>and declared a sprinkle emergency.</p>
<p><br /></p>
<p>The dinos blinked.</p>
<p><br /></p>
<p>Tomato soup dripped from trees.</p>
<p>A noodle signed Tina’s clipboard.</p>
<p><br /></p>
<p>Out in the lake, Eggasaurus floated like a ravioli.</p>
<p><br /></p>
<p>“WOBBLE SUCCESS!” he shouted,</p>
<p>spinning a soggy breadstick like a champion.</p>
<p><br /></p>
<p>Baby Bloop threw jellybeans in the air.</p>
<p><br /></p>
<p>Zoomy skated in circles yelling,</p>
<p>“Eggasaurus! Eggasaurus! King of the ketchup!”</p>
<p><br /></p>
<p>Tina sighed, still covered in cheese.</p>
<p>“I hereby declare him…</p>
<p>The Land of Wobblebottom’s Official Roller of Rescue.”</p>
<p><br /></p>
<p>Grumpasaurus grunted.</p>
<p>“Hmph. Took you long enough.”</p>
<p><br /></p>
<p>The dinos danced.</p>
<p>They jiggled.</p>
<p>They wobbled.</p>
<p>They snorted juice out their noses.</p>
<p><br /></p>
<p>Eggasaurus did the worm.</p>
<p>And the worm clapped.</p>
<p><br /></p>
<p>As the noodles napped in the sun,</p>
<p>Eggasaurus stood on a stack of muffins and proclaimed:</p>
<p><br /></p>
<p>“From this day forward… let it be known…</p>
<p>You don’t need claws or stomps or roars…</p>
<p>TO BE DINO-MIGHTY!”</p>
<p><br /></p>
<p>He paused.</p>
<p>Then added with a wink:</p>
<p><br /></p>
<p>“You don’t need to fit to shine,</p>
<p>just wobble in your own design.”</p>
<p><br /></p>
<p>He rolled into the sunset.</p>
<p>Backwards.</p>
<p>Singing the alphabet out of order.</p>
<p><br /></p>
<p>“Q... W... Marshmallow... H!”</p>
<p><br /></p>
<p>Baby Bloop waved a sock on a stick,</p>
<p>Sir Socky von Wiggle, flying proudly.</p>
<p><br /></p>
<p>The crowd cheered.</p>
<p>The birds burped bubbles.</p>
<p><br /></p>
<p>And somewhere far away,</p>
<p>a second rainbow-striped egg wobbled.</p>
<p><br /></p>
<p>It had googly eyes.</p>
<p>And a mustache.</p>
<p><br /></p>
<p>THE END</p>
<p>(or maybe... the SPLAT-TASTIC beginning?)</p>
