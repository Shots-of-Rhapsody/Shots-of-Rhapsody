<p><em>Quiet. Poetic. Told entirely in dialogue between two voices echoing through a shared past.</em></p>
<p><em>This is a story about what we keep, what we lose, and the places that remember us even when we don’t return.</em></p>
<p><strong>-</strong></p>
<p><strong>HER</strong>:</p>
<p>Your name’s not on the mailbox anymore.</p>
<p>I stood there like it still should be.</p>
<p>The doorknob still sticks.</p>
<p>The walls didn’t know how to forget you either.</p>
<p>They looked at me like I was the stranger.</p>
<p><br /></p>
<p>-</p>
<p><br /></p>
<p><strong>HIM</strong>:</p>
<p>You came back.</p>
<p>I didn’t think you would.</p>
<p>The key still works.</p>
<p>But it doesn’t sound like welcome anymore.</p>
<p>More like memory. Creaking.</p>
<p><br /></p>
<p>-</p>
<p><br /></p>
<p><strong>HER</strong>:</p>
<p>The blue sweater’s gone.</p>
<p>The one that smelled like coffee and weekend mornings.</p>
<p>Did you take it?</p>
<p>Or did it finally stop waiting?</p>
<p><br /></p>
<p>-</p>
<p><br /></p>
<p><strong>HIM</strong>:</p>
<p>It’s in the closet behind the boxes.</p>
<p>I told myself I’d donate it.</p>
<p>But I never did.</p>
<p>Like if I kept it,</p>
<p>something soft would still exist between us.</p>
<p><br /></p>
<p>-</p>
<p><br /></p>
<p><strong>HER</strong>:</p>
<p>The fridge hums in E minor.</p>
<p>Still lonely.</p>
<p>Still loyal.</p>
<p>I checked the back,</p>
<p>your jar of olives was gone.</p>
<p>You hated olives.</p>
<p>But you never threw them out.</p>
<p><br /></p>
<p>-</p>
<p><br /></p>
<p><strong>HIM</strong>:</p>
<p>I liked having one thing in there</p>
<p>that never needed me.</p>
<p>That never spoiled.</p>
<p>That stayed.</p>
<p><br /></p>
<p>-</p>
<p><br /></p>
<p><strong>HER</strong>:</p>
<p>The plant on the balcony leans too far.</p>
<p>Like it’s trying to escape the glass.</p>
<p>I wonder if it remembers</p>
<p>when we danced barefoot</p>
<p>in the hallway</p>
<p>with spoons on our noses.</p>
<p>Just to make each other laugh.</p>
<p><br /></p>
<p>-</p>
<p><br /></p>
<p><strong>HIM</strong>:</p>
<p>That was the last time I saw your full smile.</p>
<p>No shadow in it.</p>
<p>No weight.</p>
<p><br /></p>
<p>-</p>
<p><br /></p>
<p><strong>HER</strong>:</p>
<p>You were the loud one.</p>
<p>But when you hurt,</p>
<p>you went silent.</p>
<p>You disappeared</p>
<p>without ever leaving.</p>
<p><br /></p>
<p>-</p>
<p><br /></p>
<p><strong>HIM</strong>:</p>
<p>Frankie still runs to the window</p>
<p>when it rains.</p>
<p>He thinks you’re coming home.</p>
<p><br /></p>
<p>-</p>
<p><br /></p>
<p><strong>HER</strong>:</p>
<p>I threw your mug once.</p>
<p>The one with the chipped lip.</p>
<p>I don’t know why.</p>
<p>I just needed something to break</p>
<p>that wasn’t me.</p>
<p><br /></p>
<p>-</p>
<p><br /></p>
<p><strong>HIM</strong>:</p>
<p>I loved you here.</p>
<p>Even when I didn’t say it.</p>
<p>Especially then.</p>
<p>I thought you knew.</p>
<p><br /></p>
<p>-</p>
<p><br /></p>
<p><strong>HER</strong>:</p>
<p>I almost told you I’d stay.</p>
<p>I almost begged.</p>
<p>But you looked tired</p>
<p>of holding me together.</p>
<p><br /></p>
<p>-</p>
<p><br /></p>
<p><strong>HIM</strong>:</p>
<p>You were right to go.</p>
<p>I made the place too heavy.</p>
<p>I made love feel like</p>
<p>something you had to survive.</p>
<p><br /></p>
<p>-</p>
<p><br /></p>
<p><strong>HER</strong>:</p>
<p>Do you ever think,</p>
<p>if I had turned around that day,</p>
<p>would you have stopped me?</p>
<p><br /></p>
<p>-</p>
<p><br /></p>
<p><strong>HIM</strong>:</p>
<p>I would have.</p>
<p>I almost did.</p>
<p>But you were already too far</p>
<p>and I was already too late.</p>
<p><br /></p>
<p>-</p>
<p><br /></p>
<p><strong>HER</strong>:</p>
<p>I saw your name</p>
<p>in a baby announcement last fall.</p>
<p>Not mine.</p>
<p>Not ours.</p>
<p>But still, it hit me</p>
<p>like the hallway light flicking on</p>
<p>at the wrong hour.</p>
<p><br /></p>
<p>-</p>
<p><br /></p>
<p><strong>…</strong></p>
<p><br /></p>
<p>-</p>
<p><br /></p>
<p><strong>HER</strong>:</p>
<p>I found a grape stem on the balcony.</p>
<p>Dried.</p>
<p>Still shaped like a wishbone.</p>
<p>I almost made a wish,</p>
<p>but didn’t know who’d win.</p>
<p><br /></p>
<p>-</p>
<p><br /></p>
<p><strong>HIM</strong>:</p>
<p>I can’t believe I remember how your voice sounded.</p>
<p>I thought forgetting would be harder than dying.</p>
<p><br /></p>
<p>-</p>
<p><br /></p>
<p><strong>HER</strong>:</p>
<p>I left a letter in the wall.</p>
<p>Behind the light switch.</p>
<p>I don’t know if you ever found it.</p>
<p>It didn’t say much,</p>
<p>just “Don’t forget the way I held your hand</p>
<p>when you were scared.”</p>
<p><br /></p>
<p>-</p>
<p><br /></p>
<p><strong>HIM</strong>:</p>
<p>If these walls still talk,</p>
<p>I hope they lie.</p>
<p>Tell the next couple</p>
<p>we were the kind of people who made it.</p>
<p><br /></p>
<p>-</p>
<p><br /></p>
<p><strong>HER</strong>:</p>
<p>Tell them</p>
<p>we tried.</p>
<p>Tell them it wasn’t all my fault.</p>
<p>Tell them I never stopped waiting</p>
<p>to hear the lock turn.</p>
<p><br /></p>
<p>-</p>
<p><br /></p>
<p><strong>HIM</strong>:</p>
<p>If someone else moves in,</p>
<p>don’t tell them everything.</p>
<p>Keep one memory</p>
<p>just for me.</p>
<p><br /></p>
<p>-</p>
<p><br /></p>
<p><strong>HER</strong>:</p>
<p>You stopped loving me</p>
<p>before I stopped needing you.</p>
<p><br /></p>
<p>-</p>
<p><br /></p>
<p><strong>HIM</strong>:</p>
<p>I know.</p>
<p><br /></p>
<p>-</p>
<p><br /></p>
<p><strong>HER</strong>:</p>
<p>Do you remember what we used to say</p>
<p>before we locked the door?</p>
<p><br /></p>
<p>-</p>
<p><br /></p>
<p><strong>HIM</strong>:</p>
<p>“Always leave the light on.”</p>
<p><br /></p>
<p>-</p>
<p><br /></p>
<p><strong>HER</strong> (<em>final whisper</em>):</p>
<p>I still do.</p>
<p>Just not for you anymore.</p>
<p><br /></p>
<p>-</p>
<p><br /></p>
